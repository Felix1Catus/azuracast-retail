Public playlists, albums and tracks work without a login. Because of Spotify's 2026 rules, a login reads only playlists that the account owns or collaborates on (plus private ones). For any other playlist, public access is used automatically.

YouTube cookies

If YouTube blocks this server (“Sign in to confirm you're not a bot”), give it the cookies of a normal YouTube account: in a private browser window, sign in to youtube.com, export cookies.txt (Netscape format) with a cookies extension, close the window, and paste or upload it here. Use a separate account: YouTube may restrict accounts used this way. Only the youtube.com cookies are kept.
